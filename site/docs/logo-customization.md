# Logo customization

The site offers project-appropriate presets and a local PNG, JPEG, or WebP upload. Byte signatures, byte limits, dimensions, and decode results are checked before the active mark changes.

## Configuration and failure modes

Choose a preset or local file, then adjust fit, crop, focal point, and background. Conversion failure keeps the previous valid logo. Reset restores the shipped mark.

## Security and verification

Images stay in browser storage and are excluded from network, logs, exports, history, telemetry, and public records. Verify spoofed bytes, oversized and malformed images, crop and fit, persistence, corruption, reset, and every displayed size.
